Source: 100 of 111 Google reviews (4.9★), pulled via Apify on 2026-10-03. Reviewer names excluded. Quotes are verbatim.

## 10. Negative reviews: objections and risks
| Review | Risk for this campaign |
|---|---|
| 1★ (2026): "Will absolutely hound you for services when initially told no... do not recommend if you just want a price quote." | **Direct hit on this funnel.** Hidden price + "Get Quote" + fast follow-up = more price shoppers. Over-following-up on "Just pricing" leads creates more of these reviews. |
| 1★ and 2★ (2023): move-out cleans that missed areas; refund refused, only a re-clean offered; tenant lost deposit because the landlord couldn't wait. | The redo-only guarantee fails when there's a deadline. Anyone who reads reviews after the ad will see this next to "we redo it free." |
| 1★ (2024): card details required upfront before service. | Payment-upfront objection. Address it on the call or thank-you screen if the policy stands. |

Sensitive health mentions (chemotherapy, asthma, illness) are deliberately excluded from ad use.
