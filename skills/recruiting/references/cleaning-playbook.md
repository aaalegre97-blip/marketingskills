# Cleaning Company Hiring Playbook

Residential and commercial cleaning is the highest-turnover, highest-volume hiring case in home services. The funnel in `funnel-audit.md` applies; this file covers what is specific to cleaners.

## What makes cleaning different

- **Turnover is structural.** 50–100% annual turnover is common. The system has to run continuously, not when someone quits.
- **The pool is wide.** Experience is not required, so traffic is cheap and plentiful. The leaks are speed, show rate, and week one.
- **Transportation is the hidden filter.** The most common reason a cleaner no-shows an interview or a first day is a ride that fell through. Ask about it on the phone screen every time.
- **Pay-per-job versus hourly** changes who applies and who stays.
- **Background checks** and sometimes bonding create a gap between offer and start that loses people.
- **The job is physical and solitary.** Solo cleaners quit from isolation; team cleaners quit from a bad team lead.

## Pay structure

| Model | Pros | Cons | Best for |
|-------|------|------|----------|
| Hourly with guaranteed minimum | Easy to explain, attracts careful workers | Slower cleaners cost more | Recurring residential, new businesses |
| Per job / per route | Fast workers earn more, cost is predictable | Quality drops without checks, "slow day" pay fear | Established routes with tight time estimates |
| Hourly + per-job bonus | Both incentives | More to explain | Teams with quality scoring in place |
| Percentage of job | Transparent | Earnings swing with booking volume | Solo cleaners on commission-style setups |

Whatever the model, the post says one weekly number. Cleaners budget weekly.

Realistic weekly ranges vary by market. Check Indeed's salary tool and three competitor posts for the city before setting the number. Being at the 60th percentile or better locally, with guaranteed hours, usually beats being top-of-market on rate with "hours vary."

## The offer that wins cleaners

Lead with, in this order:
1. Weekly pay range and guaranteed hours
2. Schedule: Mon–Fri, start and end time, no weekends (or weekends stated with a premium)
3. Paid weekly, paid training from hour one
4. Supplies, uniform, and gas or mileage covered
5. Tips (state what cleaners actually average)
6. Raise at 90 days, lead cleaner path

Add one risk-reversal: "Paid 2-hour working interview, no obligation."

Avoid "must have own vehicle" as a hard requirement if you can route around it or pair people. It removes a large share of the pool.

## Job post

Use Template 1 in `job-post-templates.md`. Titles that pull on Indeed: "House Cleaner," "Residential Cleaner," "Housekeeper," "Cleaner." Not "Cleaning Technician," "Home Specialist," or "Team Member."

Run the post in English and Spanish where the labor market calls for it. Keep the phone screen in the applicant's language.

## Traffic

Cost per applicant for cleaners is typically $10–40. If you are above that, the post is the problem, not the market.

Sources in order of retained-hire quality:
1. **Referrals from current cleaners.** $[100–300] bonus, half at day 30 and half at day 90. Announce it monthly, not once.
2. **Indeed sponsored**, reposted every 2–3 weeks.
3. **Facebook/Instagram job ads** with a lead form, targeted by zip code, photo of a real cleaner.
4. **Local Facebook groups**, church and community boards, ESL program boards.
5. **Craigslist** in markets where it still works.

## Phone screen for cleaners

Five questions:
1. "What are you doing for work now, and why are you looking?"
2. "Can you do Mon–Fri, [8 to 4]? Anything that gets in the way: kids' schedule, another job, school?"
3. "How are you getting to the first house each day?" Dig if the answer is vague. A ride that is "usually" available is a no-show waiting to happen.
4. "The pay is [$X–Y a week], paid Fridays. Does that cover what you need?"
5. "Have you cleaned professionally before? Doesn't matter if not, we train. Just want to know where to start you."

Then book the paid working interview within 48 hours.

## The paid working interview

Two hours, paid at full rate, alongside your best lead cleaner on a real job. It replaces the sit-down interview and converts better: the applicant sees the job, you see them work, and nobody has wasted an afternoon.

Score three things: shows up on time, takes direction, pace after 30 minutes. Decide on the spot. Make the offer in the car or by text within the hour.

## Background checks and the start gap

Run the check the day of the offer. Where the law allows, start paid training while it clears. If it must clear first, say exactly how long it takes and text an update every 2 days. Silence during this gap is where cleaners take other jobs.

## Week one for cleaners

- **Day 0 text:** time, address or shop, trainer's name, what to wear, lunch, first check date.
- **Day 1–3:** paired with a named trainer with a checklist. Not "whoever's available."
- **Day 3 text from the manager:** "How's it going? Anything confusing?"
- **Day 5:** 10-minute chat. Confirm first-check math. Ask what would make them stay.
- **Week 2:** first solo house or first house as a team member. Quality check the same day.
- **Day 30:** milestone. A number (bonus or raise step) stated in the offer, paid on time.

Track retention by trainer. One bad trainer can produce most of a company's turnover.

## Retention levers that work in cleaning

- Consistent routes: the same houses, the same days. Cleaners stay for clients they like.
- Weekly pay, never biweekly.
- Tips passed through in full and shown on the stub.
- A lead-cleaner path with a real number, and people who visibly got it.
- Schedule flexibility for school hours where routes allow.
- Supplies that work. A cleaner fighting a bad vacuum all day quits.

## Numbers to watch weekly

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Applicants contacted within 15 min | 90%+ |
| Phone screen → working interview booked | 50%+ |
| Working interview show rate | 65%+ |
| Working interview → offer | 50%+ |
| Offer → start | 80%+ |
| Day-30 retention | 75%+ |
| Day-90 retention | 60%+ |
| Cost per 30-day retained hire | Under one week of that cleaner's billed revenue |

When one of these drops, go to the matching stage in `funnel-audit.md`.
